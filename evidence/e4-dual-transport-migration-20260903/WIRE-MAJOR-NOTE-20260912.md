# Interpretation note: which Wire major this campaign ran at — 2026-09-12

**This note adds nothing to the result and removes nothing from it. The raw
evidence in this directory is unchanged and must stay unchanged**; a recorded
measurement that gets edited after the fact is no longer a measurement. What
follows is a qualification of how the result may be cited, written nine days
after the run, when preparing the first public candidate.

## What was found

The ordinary Tier-0 traffic in this campaign was carried at **Wire major 0**,
the Experimental major — not at Wire major 1, the Stable one.

Two independent facts establish it, both readable in the tree:

- `mcl-sdk/tools/dual_host_shim.c` configures its node with
  `mcl_link_wire_major_mask(0u)` and sends through
  `mcl_node_send_framed_tier0()`, whose contract is fixed at
  `MCL_WIRE_EXPERIMENTAL_MAJOR` for source compatibility
  (`mcl-wire/include/mcl/wire.h`).
- the same shim populates `machine_class` in the PRESENCE it emits. That field
  **does not exist at major 1** — it was removed on the evidence recorded in
  `mcl-core/governance/MACHINE_CLASS_AUDIT.md` — so traffic carrying it is
  major-0 traffic by construction.

This was not a defect in the run and nothing about the run was wrong. At the
time it was performed, no node-level send path could emit Wire major 1 inside a
Link frame at all. That path was added on 2026-09-12
(`mcl_node_send_framed_tier0_at_major`).

## What this campaign therefore does establish

Everything it always claimed, unchanged:

- one logical contact surviving **104 physical-medium changes**, including 100
  alternating BLE/IP migrations
- candidate path validation, `COMMIT`/`CONFIRM`, retransmission recovery after a
  lost `CONFIRM`, wrong-transport refusal, and session continuity across the
  change
- at `E4 MULTI_DEVICE_OVER_AIR`, between two machines with both radios live

**The migration controls are the claim.** The Wire major of the object being
carried while the bearer changed underneath it is incidental to that claim: the
contact, the handoff sequence and the session are Link-layer and contact-layer
properties, and they behaved identically regardless of which major the payload
object used.

## What it does not establish, and must not be cited as

> Wire major 1 survived 104 migrations.

It does not show that. Nothing in this directory does. Citing it that way merges
two separate claims, and the merge is easy to make by accident because both are
true-sounding and adjacent.

## Where the Stable pair *is* evidenced

`mcl-ap/experiments/008-embedded-node/` carries a **Wire major 1 object inside a
Link major 1 frame**, modulated and decoded across a physical acoustic channel
on an ESP32-S3-class target, in both directions, on a different toolchain and a
different architecture from the host gates. That is the physical evidence for
the Stable pair.

So the layers are evidenced separately:

| Property | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Wire 1 cross-implementation | C4 / C5, 803 and 108 checks |
| Wire 1 + Link 1 across a physical medium | Experiment 008 |
| Contact migration across a physical medium | this campaign, at Wire 0 |
| **Wire 1 + Link 1 *under migration*** | **not run** |

That last row is the honest gap. It is recorded in
`EVIDENCE_INDEX.json` under `wire_major_qualification.combination_not_run` and
is in the post-v1 evidence backlog. It was deliberately **not** closed before
the first public candidate: the two-radio rig would have to be rebuilt to answer
a question whose constituent parts are already answered separately, and
reopening a frozen physical campaign to improve the phrasing of a claim is a
worse trade than stating the limit plainly.
